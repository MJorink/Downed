# Downed
A BONELAB mod that makes you go downed before dying, while downed you can get revived!
<br>
Ways to get revived: <br>
- Being grabbed by another player for 5 seconds
- Using SDK mods, for example the [Signalis Auto Injector](https://mod.io/g/bonelab/m/auto-injector-signalis), you can also self-revive with this!
- Double-pressing the right joystick (manual force-revive)

While downed you will 'bleed out' in 20 seconds, 'bleeding' will pause while being grabbed/revived. <br>
*there is no actual blood effect, you just die after 20 seconds*
